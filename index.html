FAQ

Do referral links cost me anything?

No. You get the same (sometimes better) public offer; the issuer pays the referrer separately.

Will applying hurt my credit score?

Each application adds a hard inquiry (a few points, temporary). Opening many cards in a short window can also lower your average account age. Space applications out and only apply for cards you'd actually use.
